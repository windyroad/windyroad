## Governance-iteration friction (2026-08-05 P122 AFK iter)

- **For a decision-shaped dependency, "landed" means ratified, not committed, and the two look identical from git.** P122's sequencing gate read "do not start before P120 lands". P120's code landed at `e7d115c`, so the gate looked satisfied; but what P120 shipped was ADR-043, which is still `human-oversight: unconfirmed`, and the fix would have amended its contract. The substance-confirm guard caught it, which is the system working. The cheaper move is to read a sequencing Investigation Task that names an upstream ticket as asking two questions, not one: did the code land, and is the decision it shipped ratified. `wr-architect-is-decision-unconfirmed <ADR> docs/decisions` answers the second in one call.
- **A gate axis's NAME encodes its remediation direction, and getting the name wrong licenses the opposite fix.** The candidate axis `close-discharges-headline` makes the headline the authority the body must satisfy, so the cheapest remediation is to strengthen the close until it matches the title. That is the exact claim-inflation ADR-043's skeptic differential forbids, and with no override arm it lands as an applied edit to reader-facing prose. `close-collects-the-so-what` inverts the authority to the body and makes every available remediation deflationary. The JTBD gate caught this; no other gate reads for it. When adding an axis to any auto-remediating gate, say out loud what the laziest fix for that axis would be, and rename until the lazy fix is the safe one.
- **Adding a never-remediable axis to a gate with a mechanical any-weakness-fails verdict destroys the verdict's information content.** A stop-and-surface finding survives every round by construction, so the gate can never return PASS on an edition carrying one, so the paired re-invocation fires against an unchanged body for zero delta, and the "Tom keeps overriding the verdict" reassessment criterion trips as a false positive. Two rules follow for any gate of this shape: skip the re-invocation when every collected finding is unremediable, and write the false-positive warning into the reassessment criteria so a later review does not act on it.
- **A ticket's evidence list can be wrong, not just its framing, and one `git show` settles it.** P122's Description recalled the Issue 16 item run as "court ruling, project policy, regulation-in-force, industry letters". On disk at `d2d674a` it is project policy, regulation-in-force, industry letters, court ruling, which makes the defect worse than described: the two binding rules sat either side of the item whose own heading says nothing binds anybody. Two of the eight listed findings also turned out not to reproduce at that commit at all, because they arose against intermediate revision states. Verify the evidence rows, not only the claim they support.
- **Both governance gates returning ISSUES FOUND on a well-formed strategy is the normal case, not a failure signal.** Architect and JTBD each returned five and three substantive corrections on a strategy that was already grounded on disk, and every one of them changed the answer: the amendment count went from two ADRs to three, one axis was renamed, two remediation-invariance limbs were added, and the "zero new invocations" claim was corrected to "zero for the sweep, four per issue for the loop". Budget the gate round as design work, not as approval.
