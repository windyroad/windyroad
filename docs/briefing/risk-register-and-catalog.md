## Risk Register and Catalog

> Split out of [`risk-scorer-behaviour.md`](risk-scorer-behaviour.md) on 2026-08-29 under the Tier 3 budget (6445 bytes against a 5120 ceiling). The sibling file is about how the scorer behaves as a reviewer; these two entries are about the standing-risk register and its catalog, which is a separate subject with a separate decision behind it. Both still current.

- **The risk register has two feeds and one switch, and while the switch is off the second feed fails silently.** `docs/risks/` has never existed here, which reads as "the bootstrap was never run". The quieter half: `drain-register-queue.sh` no-ops and exits 0 unless BOTH `docs/risks/` and `docs/risks/README.md` exist, so the ongoing drain no-ops on every AFK iteration reaching it. By 2026-08-23 `.afk-run-state/risk-register-queue.jsonl` held 91 hint records spanning roughly three months, gitignored and unbacked, with nothing consuming them. The asymmetry worth holding: `.risk-reports/` is pruned at seven days by the scorer's own hook, so the queue is the only durable record, and the drain *truncates* it on a successful run. A bare `mkdir docs/risks` is inert; creating the pair is the consequential act, because the next iteration reaching the drain will complete it unreviewed.

- **Do not populate the register just to clear the nudge: the slugs decide whether it helps or hurts.** The catalog protocol's primary filter tokenises a slug on hyphens and counts ANY token appearing in the diff, commit message, or prompt as a match. The queued slugs are sentence-shaped descriptions of single findings, so they behave as wildcards: measured on 2026-08-23, 71 of 72 unique queued slugs matched one ordinary commit, with `gate` in 13 of them and `without` in 11. Draining as-is would trade an honest `CATALOG_HIT_RATE: matched=0` for a near-total match that destroys the sub-30% reassessment trigger, which is the only instrument for telling whether the catalog works. An empty register costs re-derivation effort; a register that matches everything costs that AND the signal that something is wrong. ADR-056 records the admission rule (a slug must name a class, with a 25% match-rate pass mark) and the reasons the register stays unarmed until entries can meet it.
