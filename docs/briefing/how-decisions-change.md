## How a decision is changed

Split out of `governance-surface-conflicts.md` on 2026-08-23 when that file went over the Tier 3 budget. This topic will grow as ADR-054 is applied, so it gets its own file rather than crowding the conflicts topic.

- **A decision is now changed by a new decision, never by editing the old one, and the rule is not yet ratified.** `docs/decisions/054-a-decision-is-changed-by-a-new-decision-never-by-editing-the-old-one.proposed.md`, recorded 2026-08-16, ends the practice of appending an `## Amendment` section or a dated pointer to an overtaken record. Only frontmatter may change: `amends:`/`amended-by:` when part of a record is overtaken, `supersedes:`/`superseded-by:` when the whole record is retired, and the amending record must name the specific clause rather than just the record. `scripts/decisions-supersession.test.mjs` enforces both pairs in **both** directions, so a forward claim without its reciprocal goes red and so does a lone reciprocal. There is no green half: the claim and its reciprocal land in one commit or not at all. Six records carrying pre-existing `## Amendment` sections are grandfathered by a hard-coded allowlist in that test; dated-pointer blockquotes (ADR-007, ADR-008, ADR-027) are not in it and are neither caught nor protected.
