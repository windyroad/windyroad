## How a decision is changed

Split out of `governance-surface-conflicts.md` on 2026-08-23 when that file went over the Tier 3 budget. This topic will grow as ADR-054 is applied, so it gets its own file rather than crowding the conflicts topic.

- **A decision is now changed by a new decision, never by editing the old one, and the rule is not yet ratified.** `docs/decisions/054-a-decision-is-changed-by-a-new-decision-never-by-editing-the-old-one.proposed.md`, recorded 2026-08-16, ends the practice of appending an `## Amendment` section or a dated pointer to an overtaken record. Only frontmatter may change: `amends:`/`amended-by:` when part of a record is overtaken, `supersedes:`/`superseded-by:` when the whole record is retired, and the amending record must name the specific clause rather than just the record. `scripts/decisions-supersession.test.mjs` enforces both pairs in **both** directions, so a forward claim without its reciprocal goes red and so does a lone reciprocal. There is no green half: the claim and its reciprocal land in one commit or not at all. Six records carrying pre-existing `## Amendment` sections are grandfathered by a hard-coded allowlist in that test; dated-pointer blockquotes (ADR-007, ADR-008, ADR-027) are not in it and are neither caught nor protected. The record itself is `human-oversight: unconfirmed`, so work that applies it is blocked until Tom ratifies. Applied for the first time on 2026-08-24, and three things only show up in use. The test's green is narrower than it looks: it verifies DECLARED relationships, so an undeclared `amends` passes unnoticed, and citing the pass as proof a graph is complete is false assurance. Adding a reciprocal to a record that already has one produces a duplicate YAML key rather than a merged list, which silently drops a relationship; the test does catch that. And landing a record unratified is the cheap direction, not the risky one, because refusal costs a `git rm` and two reverted frontmatter lines only while none of its enforcement surfaces have landed. <!-- signal-score: 3 | last-classified: 2026-08-25 | first-written: 2026-08-23 -->
