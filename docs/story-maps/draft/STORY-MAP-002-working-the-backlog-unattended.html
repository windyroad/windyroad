Working the backlog unattended: 7 steps, 1 release.
A: Capture what went wrong We record a defect without stopping the work in hand.B: Rank what to work on next We re-score the queue and pick the next ticket with nobody in the room.C: Work the ticket unattended The loop works the ticket on its own and queues the calls it cannot make itself.D: Get the change past the gates Five gates read it: architecture, jobs, voice, risk and accessibility.E: Land the fix where the defect lives The defect often lives in an upstream plugin, so landing the fix means contributing there, not here.F: Release it and close the ticket The change reaches the people running the plugin. The ticket closes when the defect is gone, not when the fix is described.G: Read back and ratify You get the file itself, not a description, and the decision restated in the message.
