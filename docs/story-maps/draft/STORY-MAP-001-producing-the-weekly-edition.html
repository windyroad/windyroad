Producing the weekly edition: 9 steps, 1 release.
A: Collect the week's candidates We sweep the sources and gather the candidates before you read anything.B: Filter to what earns a place Two checks decide what stays: the item has to move something on the landscape map, and it has to hold up under all three lenses.C: Update the landscape map and its analysis The landscape map moves first, then the analysis. Working material only. It is never named in the body the reader sees.D: Draft the edition and its companion post Written from what changed on the landscape map.E: Put it through the gates Voice, content risk, critic, editor, skeptic, shape and structure, each one a separate read.F: Fix what the gates found A defect a gate can name is fixed before you read. A judgement call reaches you as one. Where a fix rewrote your words, you see the sentence before and after, not a summary.G: Send it for an external read You send it out for an external read. What comes back should be new. If the reviewer raises something a gate already named, fix the gate.H: Read every line and decide to publish You read every line, and publishing is your call. The pipeline leaves the edition in drafts and stops there.I: Publish, then check what went out We check the published edition against the version the gates passed.
