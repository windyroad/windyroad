changes and tool results, routable to a SIEM. It was not verified that this specifically
records auto mode's allow and deny decisions. Reduced to what the documentation shows.

because the Irregular boundary was a real network rule that was misconfigured rather than
a boundary stated in prose; and "one environment variable", which trivialised a fleet-wide
