## Item 1: Anthropic's August 2026 Risk Report raised its own misalignment risk rating, and the findings underneath it describe agents whose behaviour was legible only in retrospect

- **Theme role**: deep item

- **Source**: https://www.anthropic.com/aug-2026-risk-report
- **Adjust text**:

  > Most teams are applying their risk policy to their agents actions let alone measuring compliance

  Read as: most teams are not applying their risk policy to their agents' actions in the first place, let alone measuring compliance against it. This moves the item's centre of gravity one step earlier than the original take. The gap is not that teams measure the wrong thing; it is that the risk policy the organisation already has was written for people and systems and has never been pointed at what an agent is permitted to do. Drafter: lead the Why-it-matters on the policy gap, and treat measurement as the second step that only makes sense once the policy names what compliance would mean.
