## Declined on the record

**The actionability objection.** The skeptic raised, across several rounds, that a reader finishes the edition asking what they did differently, characterising the week as "a handful of cheap checks and a watch item".

**Declined, on two grounds, the first of which corrects the framing.**

First, the characterisation understates what the edition carries. The closing section is six numbered steps, each with a cost, split three that need nobody else's time and three that do, with explicit guidance on which can wait and why. That is a substantive action payload, not a watch item.

Second, and as general policy: The Shift exists to keep IT leaders abreast of AI news, and not every week produces significant actions. JTBD-005's fourth outcome is confidence the reader is not missing a shift their team should be acting on, which a genuinely quiet week delivers rather than fails. The persona constraint of little time to keep up personally argues against manufacturing actions to fill a thin week.

**Scope on that second ground**, so it is not read more broadly than intended: it licenses no action payload, not unearned length. JTBD-005 outcome 1 as amended requires a read whose length is earned by what it returns, and three ratified editor axes hang off it. A quiet week still has to be proportionate.

**Follow-up requiring Tom's ratification, not actioned here.** The JTBD reviewer established that recording this policy in `.claude/skills/wr-newsletter/personas/leader.md` will not work. The skeptic gate receives only `artifact_path`, `artifact_kind`, a bare `persona` label and `edition_number`, and never sees the persona config, so a note there changes the drafter and leaves the gate re-raising the objection every quiet week. The objection is also emitted from the gate's explicitly unbounded sharpest-objection section, designed not to be suppressible by rubric text. The corpus has settled this shape twice, at ADR-043 line 196 and in the 2026-08-10 JTBD-005 amendment, and both say the fix is to add the outcome to the job file. So the amendment belongs in `docs/jtbd/engineering-leader/JTBD-005-stay-ahead-of-the-shift.proposed.md`, dated, carrying the outcome-1 scoping above, and it is a re-ratification ask. A pointer in `leader.md` is a reciprocal change, not the home.
