## Process note

Two failure modes ran through this pass and both are worth recording, because
neither was caught by the drafter.

The first is remediation claims that were not true. Five times, a fix was
described to a gate as applied when it was not on disk; on the worst occasion the
edit had never been written at all, and the drafter reported it as landed to three
separate gates. Ten further times a gate corrected the drafter's description of
its own edits, on counts, on word totals, or on changes made but not mentioned.
Every one of these was caught by a gate re-reading the file, never by the drafter
re-reading their own work. The countermeasure adopted was to freeze the brief
under an md5 each round and ask every gate to verify against disk rather than
against the handoff.

The second is fixes that introduced fresh defects. The opener's closing passage
was rewritten four times and each of the first three rewrites introduced a new
error that the skeptic gate caught: a false consequence, a claim of testability
for an untestable property, and a durability claim that contradicted the
edition's own second cut. The pattern was always the same shape, which the critic
gate named: a claim corrected locally without its counterpart elsewhere in the
edition being revisited.

Rounds 18 and 19 added a third mode and sharpened the second. The third is a
remediation that is itself a regression: a sentence was cut back to remove an
over-claim and the cut removed the head noun that made the referent resolve, so
the claim got safer and the sentence got harder. The gate that caught it was the
one measuring comprehension, not the one that had asked for the reduction, which
is the argument for running both after an edit rather than only the gate that
raised the finding. The sharpening is that the some-sites-not-all pattern turned
out to be symmetric. Having chased it four times on one side of the edition's
central comparison, the drafter fixed three sites and left the fourth standing on
the other side, where it was not being watched. A correction has as many sites as
the claim has, not as many as the drafter has been looking at.

Two standing habits came out of it, both from the critic gate, and both are
recommended for future editions. Treat any closed enumeration ("four things",
"three standing limits") as a contract, and re-read it whenever a property is
added or removed anywhere in the draft. And when a paragraph draws a conclusion
that depends on a distinction made earlier, name the distinction at the point of
use rather than assuming it.

Round 20 supplied the reason the drafter's countermeasure kept failing, and it
generalises past this edition. Having been caught three times applying a
correction at some sites and not all, the drafter ran a grep sweep for both
patterns across the whole brief before the final round. The sweep still missed the
sharpest site, in the opener. Two reasons, from the skeptic. First, the same
property was asserted in five different surface forms across one edition, so no
single string enumerates them, and the missed site hid behind the word "shift",
which is this publication's own name, so the real hit drowned in the masthead and
the file path. Second, and the general point: the defect is not carried by a
keyword at all. It is carried by the contrast frame, and the unearned claim lives
in the implied comparison class, which no regex can see. The pass that works is an
enumeration rather than a search. Listing every sentence whose subject is a check
and whose predicate is an invariance, an unarguability or a staffing property
produced thirteen candidates, eleven real, and each was settled in one pass. That
ended a four-round streak. It is captured as a ticket.

One measurement gap is recorded rather than repaired. The cognitive-accessibility
gate measures each round against the previous round's numbers, and no measurement
was taken for round 17. Its round 18 comparison therefore spans two rounds of
edits, and the drafter's change description named three changed lines against
fourteen the gate found on disk. The gate said so, and its two most severe
findings that round both sat in edits the description had not mentioned. A gate
that re-derives the change set from disk rather than trusting the handoff is the
reason that was survivable.
